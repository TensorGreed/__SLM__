Definition

Small is relative.

A language model designed to be smaller, cheaper, faster, or easier to deploy than a big general-purpose model.

  • Fewer parameters
  • Lower memory use
  • Shorter context window
  • Narrower task scope
  • Smaller deployment target — laptop, phone, edge device
There is no hard cutoff between LLM and SLM. It's a spectrum.
BrewSLM · Video 01
Why Smaller Models Matter

Five reasons.

  • Lower serving cost. No H-100 fleet required.
  • Lower latency. Small models respond in milliseconds.
  • Easier on-device deployment. Fits on a laptop or phone.
  • Better control for narrow tasks. Tuned models beat general-purpose ones on their job.
  • Smaller blast radius. Failures are contained.
