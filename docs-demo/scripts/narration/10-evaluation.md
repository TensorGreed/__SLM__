## Pre-roll (not narrated)

Playwright logs in as `admin`, launches the Support FAQ sample, checks that `v09-narrated-run` is completed, clicks **Results** (`/project/<id>/results?tab=verdict`) and waits for the summary card.

## Section 1 — Cold open, Results · Verdict (~00:00)

**On screen**: spotlight the workflow tabs, then the summary card.

> Now we score the model we just trained. We are on Results, the page
> that answers three questions: did it work, where does it fail, and how
> does it compare. Verdict is the first tab, and its summary card sits
> at the top. We will score the run against the practice answer key.
> That is two hundred rows with hand-written answers, never seen during
> training.

## Section 2 — Setup, the Evaluate form (~00:28)

**On screen**: click **+ Evaluate on test examples**; pick *Practice answer key* and *Exact Match*; spotlight the form.

> Under Verdict, the button Evaluate on test examples opens the form.
> Pick the dataset — the test examples, the validation split, or the
> practice or final answer key — and a metric: exact match, F1, or an
> LLM judge. The model generates an answer for each question, and the
> handler scores it against the expected one. Here it is the practice
> answer key, scored by exact match, with token- level F1 reported
> beside it as a more forgiving second number.

## Section 3 — Kickoff (~01:02)

**On screen**: the spec POSTs `run-heldout` through the API (non-blocking; 20 samples, 64 new tokens, temperature 0).

> I launch the evaluation through the API, so the recording stays
> repeatable. Twenty samples keep it short, at most sixty-four new
> tokens each, and no sampling randomness. The same call works against
> all two hundred rows. The practice answer key is for iterating. The
> final answer key stays untouched until you are ready to report a
> number you can trust.

## Section 4 — Watching (~01:28)

**On screen**: await the request, reload `/results?tab=verdict`, spotlight the summary card.

> The evaluation loads the trained checkpoint once, generates an answer
> for each sample, scores each one, and aggregates. On this hardware
> that is about ten seconds for twenty samples. Reload Verdict, and the
> card reads the new result.

## Section 5 — Results (~01:44)

**On screen**: Verdict: spotlight the **Pass/fail rules** card, then the scorecard; then the **Failures** tab and the sample predictions.

> Done. Exact match is zero. The model is too small to reproduce an
> expected answer word for word. F1 is around a quarter: real word
> overlap with the expected answers, and nowhere near production
> quality. The pass/fail rules fail, honestly, and the scorecard shows
> which rule failed and by how much. Then Failures: the sample
> predictions, each question next to the expected answer and what the
> model actually said. That is where you would start reading.

## Section 6 — Wrap (~02:17)

**On screen**: hold on the sample predictions.

> The result is now stored against the run. The score is not the point.
> The loop closed, and we can see exactly where the model falls short,
> row by row. The next video compresses the trained adapter into a small
> file that is ready to serve.

- Don't promise a specific score; the 2026-10-18 take read exact match 0, F1 0.24 on 20 samples.
- The summary card picks the latest completed run; the video assumes Video 09's run is the newest.
